Our Services

Data cleaning

Here’s exactly what I handle: Deduplication: Finding and removing exact and fuzzy duplicates (even if names are spelled differently). Standardization: Fixing date formats, address formats, and capitalization so everything matches. Missing Data: Intelligently filling in blanks or flagging gaps for your review. Validation: Checking emails, phone numbers, and IDs against proper formats. Merging: Combining multiple files with different column names into one master dataset.

Data Analysis

I turn complex healthcare data into clear, actionable narratives for better patient outcomes and smarter operations. Healthcare generates massive amounts of data—electronic health records, clinical trial results, insurance claims, and lab reports. But this data is often siloed, messy, and difficult to interpret. My service bridges the gap between raw healthcare data and real-world decisions. I clean, integrate, and analyze clinical and claims data, then translate the findings into compelling stories that help hospitals, researchers, and life sciences companies improve care, reduce costs, and accelerate innovation.

AI

I build machine learning models that predict patient outcomes by transforming raw healthcare data into actionable clinical insights. My service covers the complete pipeline: cleaning and integrating electronic health records, claims, and lab data; engineering predictive features; and training algorithms including Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Survival Models. I rigorously evaluate performance using AUC-ROC, precision, recall, and F1-score, and ensure model transparency with SHAP and LIME for clinical interpretability. I package models for deployment via FastAPI or Flask. Predictions address readmission risk, sepsis onset, mortality, adverse drug events, length of stay, and deterioration. I deliver clean datasets, performance reports, visualizations, and deployable endpoints with full documentation.
